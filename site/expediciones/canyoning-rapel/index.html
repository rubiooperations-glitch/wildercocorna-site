Waterfall abseiling (rappelling)

Adventure 4/5

Abseil down a waterfall in Cocorná. Two descents: start with the 36-metre drop, then carry on down the 42-metre one if you want more.

Heights36 and 42 m
ForAll levels
IncludesGear and guide
DurationHalf day

What's included

  • Harness, helmet and ropes

Not included

Before you book

  • The 42 m descent comes after the 36 m one: to do the 42 m you always do both.
  • Price and availability depend on the date and group size: we'll quote you on WhatsApp.
  • In bad weather we reschedule or offer you an alternative.
  • You can combine it with a hike or with your stay in Cocorná.
