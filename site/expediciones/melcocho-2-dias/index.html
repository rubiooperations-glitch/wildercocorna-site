Jungle Trekking Melcocho, 2 days / 1 night

Adventure 4/5

The short version of the Melcocho: the river canyon, its crystal-clear pools and a night at a family farm. Ideal if you have a weekend.

Duration2 days / 1 night
Start8:00 a.m., fixed
LevelDemanding
Meals4 included

Closed one week a month for river cleaning. Ask for available dates on WhatsApp.

What it's like

You sleep on small farms run by country families who cook wonderfully and make you feel at home. Often in the children's rooms: the kids have moved out and the grandparents still live on the farm. Simple beds, rural and basic stays, and that is exactly the experience. Your stay also really helps these families.

For the trail they pack you a fiambre: a traditional lunch wrapped in a banana leaf, which you eat straight from the leaf.

Along the way you pass streams and waterfalls, with pools to swim in.

Bring: swimwear and a change of clothes.

About the river: where the name Melcocho comes from, the protected canyon and the community rules, on elmelcocho.com.

What's included

  • Local guide for the whole route
  • Entrance fees
  • 4 meals: lunch and dinner (day 1), breakfast and lunch (day 2)
  • 1 night at a family farm

Not included

  • Transport to the starting point (we can quote it)
  • Drinks and personal expenses

Itinerary

StartEl Retiro

  1. Day 1

    Start at 8:00 a.m. Hike through the Melcocho river canyon, swimming in natural pools. Lunch on the way, dinner and night at a family farm.

  2. Day 2

    Breakfast at the farm, a last swim in the river, lunch and the hike back.

FinishEl Retiro

Before you book

  • All hikes are demanding. There is no minimum age: it depends on your experience and fitness. If in doubt, message us.
  • In bad weather we reschedule or offer you an alternative.
  • We sleep at authentic family farms: simple and clean, not hotels.
